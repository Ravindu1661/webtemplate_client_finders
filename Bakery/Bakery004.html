Our story

The art of traditional baking.

Maison Levain began with one small oven, a family sourdough starter and an insistence on doing things properly. Two decades later, we still shape every loaf by hand and wait for flavour to arrive in its own time.

Our bakers work with stone-ground flour, seasonal produce and producers we know by name — a quieter kind of luxury, made fresh every day.
