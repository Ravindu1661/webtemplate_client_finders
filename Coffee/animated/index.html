Our Story

A Legacy Rooted in Craft & Passion

Born from an obsession with perfection, NOIR began as a single roastery dedicated to sourcing the world's rarest beans. Today, we remain devoted to the same philosophy — patience, precision, and an unwavering respect for the bean's journey from soil to cup.

Every batch is roasted in small quantities, tasted by our masters, and served with an intention to transform the ordinary into something extraordinary.
