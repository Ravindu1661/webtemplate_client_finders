Our Philosophy

A sanctuary built
around the bean.

NOIR & GOLD was founded on a single idea: that coffee deserves the same reverence as fine wine. Every element of our house — the light, the sound, the silence between sips — is designed to slow you down and let the roast speak.

We work directly with fewer than twenty micro-farms across Ethiopia, Panama and Colombia, roasting in small batches inside our Geneva atelier every single morning.

The Bean Journey

01. Origin

Hand-picked cherries from single-estate farms at 1,800m altitude.

02. Roasting

Slow-roasted in small batches to unlock caramel and stone-fruit notes.

03. Grinding

Precision-milled seconds before brewing to preserve aromatic oils.

04. Brewing

Extracted at exact temperature and pressure for a flawless pour.
