Information

Got Questions?

What are your opening hours?
We are open 24/7 for Pro, Elite, and VIP members. Starter members have access during off-peak hours (10:00 AM - 4:00 PM and 9:00 PM - 5:00 AM). Staffed hours are 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM daily.
Do you offer a free trial?
Yes, local residents can claim a complimentary 3-day VIP pass to experience all our facilities, including one free group class and a basic fitness assessment.
Is personal training included?
Personal training is included in the Elite (2 sessions/mo) and VIP (Unlimited) packages. For other plans, PT sessions can be purchased separately in blocks.
Can I freeze my membership?
Yes. Members can freeze their account for up to 3 months per calendar year due to travel, medical reasons, or personal choice with a 7-day notice.
