Frequently
Asked Questions

What is included in the rate?
Our standard rate includes luxury accommodation, daily housekeeping, 24/7 concierge service, high-speed WiFi, and access to all private villa amenities. Chef and driver services can be added.
Do you accommodate private events?
Yes, many of our larger estates are perfect for intimate weddings, corporate retreats, and private celebrations. Please contact our event specialists for tailored arrangements.
What is the cancellation policy?
We offer a full refund for cancellations made up to 30 days before arrival. Cancellations within 30 days are non-refundable but may be eligible for credit toward a future stay.
Are the villas pet-friendly?
Select properties are pet-friendly. Please inform our concierge team during booking so we can recommend the perfect villa for you and your furry companions.
