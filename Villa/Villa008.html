Casa Forma

Santorini, Greece

Available / August 2026

01 / Overview

An exacting white-stone retreat stepped into the caldera, composed around light, water and the Aegean horizon.

02 / Architecture

Designed as a sequence of shaded thresholds, terraces and framed views. The house is practical in the heat and generous at dusk.

03 / Services & rules

Private chef and daily housekeeping are included. Check-in after 16:00, check-out before 10:00. A considered house rulebook is shared before arrival; flexible cancellation is available up to 60 days before travel.

04 / Guest note

“There was a particular quiet to every room. It never felt like a rental; it felt like someone had paid attention.” — E.R., Copenhagen
