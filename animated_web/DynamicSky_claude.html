About the engine

One clock drives everything you see.

A single simulated clock — accelerated far beyond real time — drives a layered lighting engine: sky gradients, sun and moon position, shadow length, glass opacity, and every accent color on the page are re‑computed every frame from that one number.

Nine keyframed moments — Midnight, Sunrise, Morning, Noon, Golden Hour, Sunset, Blue Hour, Night and back to Midnight — are smoothly interpolated, so nothing ever jumps: the world eases from one mood into the next.
