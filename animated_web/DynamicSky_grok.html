The Concept

Time as a Medium

DynamicSky is not a website that shows the weather. It is a continuous simulation of a full day cycle. The sun arcs across the sky. The moon rises. Clouds drift. Colors shift. Every interface element — buttons, glass, text, shadows — responds to the living light around it.

You are not browsing a page. You are inhabiting a world that never stops changing.

Living Systems

Everything Reacts

From volumetric sky to reactive glass surfaces, every layer of this experience is driven by a single 24-hour lighting engine.

24-Hour Lighting Engine

Smooth interpolation of sky gradients, ambient color, accent tones, and shadow intensity across eight distinct phases of the day.

Dynamic Atmosphere

Volumetric clouds, fog layers, weather events, and particle systems that respond to the current phase and random atmospheric conditions.

Celestial Bodies

Realistic sun and moon arcs, lens flare, moonlight glow, rotating stars, shooting stars, and occasional aurora at night.

Reactive UI

Glass opacity, button colors, card glow, typography contrast, and shadows all update in real time as the simulated light changes.

Reflective Ocean

Animated water surface that mirrors the current sky palette, with sun and moon reflections and gentle wave motion.

Ambient Soundscape

Optional layered audio that shifts with the day — birds at dawn, wind, ocean, crickets, and night insects.
