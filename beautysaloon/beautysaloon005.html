Our Story

The Art of European Beauty

Founded in the heart of Mayfair, London, Éclat Luxe was born from a passion for timeless European elegance and the pursuit of personalised beauty excellence. Our philosophy blends the refined artistry of Parisian maisons with the precision of Swiss aesthetics and Italian sensuality.

Every treatment is a ritual — carefully crafted by master artists who have trained under the world’s leading institutes. We believe luxury is not excess; it is the quiet confidence of perfect details, the warmth of genuine care, and results that feel effortless.
