FAQ

Common Questions

How do I book an appointment?

You can book online via our website booking form, call us directly, or book through our Instagram DMs. For same-day availability, we recommend calling. We confirm all bookings within 30 minutes.

What products do you use?

We exclusively use internationally certified luxury brands including Olaplex, Kérastase, La Mer Skincare, and OPI Nails. All products are ethically sourced, cruelty-free and dermatologically tested.

Do you offer bridal packages?

Yes! Our VIP Diamond Package is perfect for brides. We also offer fully customisable bridal packages including pre-wedding consultations, hair trials, makeup trials, and full bridal party packages for your entire wedding party.

What is your cancellation policy?

We require 24 hours' notice for individual appointments and 48 hours for packages. Late cancellations or no-shows may incur a 50% service charge. For VIP bookings, 72 hours' notice is required.

Do you offer home visits or mobile services?

Yes. Our VIP mobile concierge service is available for weddings, corporate events, hotels and private residences. Please contact us directly to discuss your requirements and availability.
