Questions

Frequently Asked

What are the check-in and check-out times?
Check-in is from 3:00 PM and check-out is at 12:00 PM. Early check-in and late check-out can be arranged upon request.
Does the hotel offer airport transfers?
Yes, we offer private luxury transfers from Nice Airport. Please contact our concierge to arrange your transportation.
Is the hotel pet-friendly?
We welcome small pets in select suites. A pet concierge service is available upon request.
What dining options are available?
Our Michelin-starred restaurant, a casual terrace bistro, and 24-hour in-room dining are all available.
Can I host a private event at the hotel?
Absolutely. We offer exclusive event spaces for weddings, corporate retreats, and private celebrations.
