The Philosophy

A hotel that keeps time with the light, not the clock

Eighteen rooms are carved into a single cliff face above the Tyrrhenian Sea. There is no rush here — mornings arrive slowly through linen curtains, afternoons are for the pool and the page of a book, and by night the terraces glow like lanterns strung along the rock.

Destination

Praiano, between Positano and Amalfi

Quieter than its famous neighbours, Praiano is a fifteen-minute drive from Positano and twenty from Amalfi town — close enough for a day trip, far enough to keep its own rhythm.

  • Path of the Gods hiking trail — 20 min walk
  • Positano centre — 15 min by boat
  • Amalfi Cathedral — 25 min by car
  • Capri day trip — private boat available

Good to Know

Frequently Asked

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in from 3pm, check-out by 11am. Early check-in and late check-out are arranged when the calendar allows.

Is the property suitable for children?

We welcome children over 12 in the main house and all ages in the Ocean Villa, which has its own gated pool.

Do you arrange transfers from Naples airport?

Yes — private car or boat transfers can be arranged by our concierge at the time of booking.

Is the hotel open year-round?

We open from April through late October, when the coast and the sea are at their best.
