Frequently Asked Questions

Does the treatment hurt?
No. We use state-of-the-art computer-controlled anesthesia (The Wand) and offer various sedation options to ensure a completely pain-free and relaxing experience.
How long do porcelain veneers last?
With proper care and regular check-ups, our premium porcelain veneers can last 15-20 years. They are highly resistant to staining and chipping.
Do you offer payment plans?
Yes, we offer flexible 0% interest financing plans up to 24 months to help make your dream smile accessible.
What happens in the first consultation?
Your first visit includes a comprehensive examination, 3D scanning, digital photography, and a detailed discussion of your goals with our specialists.
