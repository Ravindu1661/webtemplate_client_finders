Our Story

A Legacy of Passion
& Perfection

Founded in 1998 by Chef Alessandro Moretti, LUXE was born from a simple belief — that extraordinary food can transform an ordinary evening into an unforgettable memory. Nestled in the heart of Paris, we have spent over two decades curating an experience that marries classical French technique with the boldest flavours of the Mediterranean.

Every dish is a canvas. Every ingredient is chosen with intention. Every guest is treated like royalty. This is not just a restaurant — this is LUXE.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do you require a dress code?

Yes. LUXE operates a smart elegant dress code. We ask that gentlemen wear a jacket and that all guests dress in a manner befitting our luxurious atmosphere.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend 2–3 weeks for weekends, 1 week for weekdays. For private events or groups of 6+, please contact us at least 4 weeks prior.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely. We accommodate vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, Halal, and Kosher menus. Please note requirements when booking.

Do you offer private dining or events?

Yes — three exclusive private dining rooms, each fully customisable with bespoke menus. Contact our events team for details and pricing.

Is there a tasting menu available?

Yes. Chef Alessandro's 9-course tasting menu is available Tue–Sat evenings, paired with a curated wine flight by our Head Sommelier. Pre-booking essential.
