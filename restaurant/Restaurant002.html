Questions

Frequently Asked

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?
Absolutely. Our kitchen prepares vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and allergen-conscious dishes. Please note your requirements when reserving so our chefs can craft a tailored experience.
Is there a dress code?
We request smart elegant attire. Jackets are preferred for gentlemen in the main dining room and private spaces. Resort wear is welcome on the rooftop garden.
How far in advance should I book?
For weekend evenings and private rooms we recommend booking 2–3 weeks ahead. Weekday lunches are often available with shorter notice. Large events require 4–6 weeks.
Do you offer tasting menus?
Yes. Our Chef's Journey is a 7-course tasting menu that changes with the seasons. Wine pairing is available, guided by our head sommelier.
Is parking available?
Complimentary valet parking is provided for all guests. Electric vehicle charging stations are available on request.
