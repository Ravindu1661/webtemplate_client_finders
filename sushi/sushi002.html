The Philosophy

Where Tradition Meets Vision

✦ 20 Years of Mastery
Since 2008

Every grain of rice, every slice of fish — a meditation.

At Sushi Kokoro, we honor the centuries-old traditions of Edomae sushi while embracing the subtle innovations of modern Japanese cuisine. Our chefs train for over a decade before they are entrusted with the omakase experience.

“Sushi is not about the fish. It is about the balance.”

From the precise temperature of the rice to the delicate hand-pressure of each nigiri, every detail is orchestrated to create a harmony that lingers long after the last bite.

The Journey

Omakase Experience

A curated tasting journey of seasonal perfection.

Course One

Zensai · 前菜

A delicate opening of seasonal vegetables, lightly pickled, with a whisper of yuzu and dashi. Served with a single, pristine oyster from Miyagi.

Course Two

Nigiri · 握り

Five exquisite pieces of nigiri, each a masterpiece of texture and flavor. From the buttery Ōtoro to the bright, clean taste of Kohada.

Course Three

Sashimi · 刺身

The purest expression of the sea — paper-thin slices of Hirame, Kanpachi, and creamy Uni, served on a bed of shiso leaf with fresh wasabi.

Course Four

Special Roll · 特製巻物

Our signature roll — a delicate balance of crab, avocado, and spicy tuna, wrapped in a whisper-thin layer of daikon, finished with gold leaf.

Course Five

Chef's Signature · 匠の一品

A secret creation, revealed only at the counter. A symphony of textures — crisp, silky, and umami-rich — crafted from the day's finest catch.

Finale

Dessert · 甘味

A whisper of sweetness — homemade matcha ice cream with red bean, or a delicate yuzu sorbet, cleansing the palate and completing the journey.
