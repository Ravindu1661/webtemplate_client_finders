The Master

Chef Kenjiro

With over 30 years of experience originating from the legendary fish markets of Tokyo, Chef Kenjiro brings an uncompromising dedication to the craft of sushi. His philosophy revolves around 'Shun' — capturing ingredients at their absolute peak of flavor.

"Sushi is simple, which is why it is so difficult. There is nowhere to hide. The rice, the vinegar, the fish, the temperature — every element must be in perfect harmony."
